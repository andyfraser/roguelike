# Roguelike User Manual

Welcome to the dungeon! This manual provides everything you need to know to survive and thrive in this terminal-based Roguelike.

## Getting Started

When you launch the game, you will be prompted to **Select Your Class**. Use the arrow keys or **W/S** to navigate and press **Enter** to confirm your choice.

### Choose Your Hero

- **Warrior:** A stalwart fighter with high health and a powerful melee attack. Best for players who want to jump straight into the fray.
- **Mage:** A master of the arcane. While physically weaker, the Mage possesses high Mana and the ability to cast devastating Fireballs.
- **Rogue:** A versatile and balanced character. Great for those who want a mix of survivability and tactical flexibility.

## Controls

The game is played entirely through your keyboard.

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Arrow Keys / WASD** | Move your character or attack an adjacent monster |
| **f / F** | Cast **Fireball** (Mage only - costs 10 Mana) |
| **h / ?** | Open the in-game help screen |
| **q / Q** | Quit the game |

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Combat

Combat is straightforward: **bump into an enemy to attack it.** If you are adjacent to a monster after your move, they will take the opportunity to strike back!

### Field of View

The dungeon is shrouded in darkness. You can only see tiles that are within your character's immediate line of sight. Tiles you have previously visited will remain visible but "dimmed" in your memory.

### Items

Keep an eye out for the **'!'** symbol. These are Health Potions that will restore 10 HP when you step on them.

### Progression

Defeating monsters grants you Experience Points (XP). The amount of XP needed to reach the next level increases as you grow stronger (**100 * Current Level**).

When you **Level Up**:
- Your **Maximum Health and Mana** increase significantly.
- Your **Attack Power** improves, making your basic attacks more lethal.
- Your **Special Skills** (like Melee and Fireball) are enhanced, dealing more damage to match tougher foes.
- Your Health and Mana are fully restored.

## Survival Tips

1. **Watch the Log:** Pay attention to the message log at the bottom of the screen. It will tell you exactly how much damage you're taking and dealing.
2. **Use the Mage's Fireball:** If you're playing as a Mage, use your Fireball ('f') to hit multiple enemies at once before they can reach you.
3. **Rest and Recover:** Pick up Health Potions whenever you find them; you'll need them for the tougher monsters on deeper levels.
4. **Know When to Retreat:** If your HP is low, try to lure monsters into narrow corridors where only one can attack you at a time.

## Game Over

If your Health reaches 0, your journey ends. However, death is not the final exit! You will be prompted with a **"Play again? (y/n)"** message. 
- Press **'y'** to return to the Class Selection screen and start a new adventure.
- Press **'n'** or any other key to exit the game.

Good luck, adventurer!
